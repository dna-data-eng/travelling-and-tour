Refund Policy

Last updated: [Date]

This policy explains how refunds are handled for services booked through Akwantufuo Travel Consult.

1. Consultancy Fees

Fees paid for consultancy work already carried out (application preparation, document review, submissions made) are non-refundable once that work has started.

2. Third-Party Fees

Visa fees, airline tickets and institutional application fees are paid directly to those third parties and follow their own refund rules — we will guide you through requesting a refund from them where one is available, but cannot guarantee it.

3. Cancellations By The Client

If you cancel before work has started on your file, fees paid to Akwantufuo Travel Consult directly are refundable minus any costs already incurred on your behalf.

4. Requesting A Refund

Contact us at akwantufuo@outlook.com or +233 33 209 7330 with your booking details to start a refund request.
